Global Fintech Fest 2026: What Financial Leaders Asked Us at Booth JY18

We exhibited at Global Fintech Fest 2026 in Mumbai and spent three days showing banks, asset managers and market institutions how AI agents can work inside their own systems.

Dates
September 9 to 11, 2026
Venue
Jio World Centre, Mumbai
Format
Conference, exhibitor at booth JY18
Organizers
PCI, NPCI and Fintech Convergence Council

About the Event

Global Fintech Fest is one of the largest fintech events in the world, and its 2026 edition at the Jio World Centre was built around agentic AI, tokenization and quantum computing. Regulators were more visible than ever, which made it a good place to hear how Indian institutions think about adopting AI responsibly.

We set up at booth JY18 in Jasmine Hall to meet the people who run research, operations, risk and technology at banks, asset managers, brokers and market infrastructure institutions. Most visitors brought a real problem with them. These are the themes that came up most often.

Six Things We Heard in Mumbai

  1. Agentic AI Was the Theme of the Event

    AI agents were everywhere on the program, and India's payments infrastructure launched its own platforms for building them. At our booth, the questions were practical: which workflows an agent can take on today, and who signs off on its output.

  2. Regulators Expect Accountability

    India's regulators made it clear that firms remain responsible for the data they hold and the AI they use. Frameworks for managing model risk and governing AI in finance are on the way, and institutions are preparing for them now.

  3. Data Governance Is a Leadership Issue

    Heads of data asked how an AI system respects the licensing terms on market data and keeps a record of everything it reads. Who owns data, and how its use is overseen, are now discussed at the leadership level.

  4. Asset Managers Want Complete Workflows

    Many managers had tried general AI assistants for research and found them limited. They wanted to see complete workflows: pulling figures from filings into their own Excel templates, tracking an investment thesis and writing reports in their house format.

  5. Tokenization Is Becoming Real

    India unveiled its first tokenized corporate bond pilot during the event. For middle and back office teams, this brings new data flows to reconcile, and several visitors asked how AI agents could help with that work.

  6. A Live Demo Answers More Than a Slide

    The best conversations started when visitors described a real task and watched us run it on filings, broker research and internal documents. Their next questions were about deployment: whether Pascal runs in their own cloud or on premises, which models it uses and how quickly they could go live.

Our Take

Mumbai confirmed that Indian institutions are asking the same questions as firms in New York, London and Hong Kong. Pascal Agents are built to answer them, working from a context graph each firm owns and running inside its own environment.

Thank you to everyone who visited booth JY18.
