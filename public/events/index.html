About Pascal AI Labs

Pascal AI Labs

Pascal builds context-driven AI agents for institutional finance, working across the front, middle and back office for hedge funds, asset managers and asset servicers.

Frequently Asked Questions

What events does Pascal AI Labs host?
Invite-only roundtables, dinners and breakfasts for leaders at hedge funds, asset managers and the firms that serve them, often co-hosted with partners.
Which conferences does Pascal attend?
Industry forums including Global Fintech Fest, Global Alts Miami, InvestOps Asia, the AIMA APAC Annual Forum and MFA Ops.
How can I attend a Pascal roundtable?
Our roundtables are invite-only. Book a demo or contact the Pascal team to be considered for upcoming events.
Are the roundtable discussions attributed?
No. Our roundtables run under the Chatham House Rule, so recaps share themes without naming who said what.
